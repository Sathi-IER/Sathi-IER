About

I'm completing an M.Ed. in Educational Psychology & Guidance at the University of Dhaka (B.Ed. Honours, CGPA 3.78/4.00), working at the meeting point of teaching, psychological measurement, and student counselling. My recent research studies how university students actually use AI tools, and adapts a metacognition scale for ChatGPT-assisted learning — both built on the same toolkit of questionnaire design and SPSS-based analysis. Alongside this, I'm training toward becoming a psychological therapist and counselor.
